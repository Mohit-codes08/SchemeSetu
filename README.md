---

## 📌 Overview

Millions of eligible Indian citizens miss out on crucial welfare schemes due to complex bureaucratic jargon, lack of digital awareness, and fragmented portals. **SchemeSetu** bridges this gap through an intuitive, multi-step smart profiler equipped with **Voice AI (Speech-to-Text)**, **Dynamic Business Goal Mapping**, and **Real-time Eligibility Algorithms**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🎙️ **Multilingual Voice Profiling:** Built-in Speech-to-Text supporting Hindi (`hi-IN`) and Indian English (`en-IN`) for accessible, hands-free citizen onboarding.
* ⚡ **Dynamic Goal Recommendation:** Intelligently maps occupation types (Farmers, Artisans, Street Vendors, Youth, Women Entrepreneurs) to relevant business initiatives (PM Vishwakarma, PM SVANidhi, PMEGP, Mudra).
* 🎯 **Smart Eligibility Engine:** Computes complex eligibility criteria across Income limits, Caste categories, Gender, and Occupation to filter actionable schemes.
* 🌐 **Bilingual Interface:** Real-time dynamic language toggle between Hindi and English without page reload.
* 📱 **Mobile-First & Responsive:** Modern cyber-grid interface built for high conversion on low-end mobile devices and desktops alike.
* 🔒 **Cloud Native Architecture:** Fully decoupled architecture with Vercel frontend, Render backend, and MongoDB Atlas database.
